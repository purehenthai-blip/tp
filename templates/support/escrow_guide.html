Escrow Guide

How your funds are protected on every transaction.

1️⃣ You Pay

When you purchase, your wallet balance is deducted and locked in escrow. The vendor cannot access it yet.

2️⃣ Vendor Delivers

The vendor fulfills your order — digital download, physical shipment, or service. You'll receive a notification.

3️⃣ You Verify

Click "Verify Delivery" on your order. Funds are instantly released to the vendor minus the 8% platform commission.

⏱️ Auto-Release

If you don't verify within 14 days of delivery confirmation, funds auto-release to the vendor. This is configurable by admin.

Dispute

If something is wrong — item not received, not as described, fraud — open a dispute from your order page. Funds stay locked until an admin resolves it.
