How does escrow work?

When you buy something, your crypto is locked in escrow. Once you verify the delivery, funds are released to the vendor. If something goes wrong, open a dispute — admin mediates.

How do I deposit crypto?

Go to Wallet, find your deposit address for your chosen currency, send from your external wallet, then open a support ticket with your transaction hash. Admin will confirm and credit your balance.

How do I become a vendor?

Go to Vendors → Apply. Submit your shop name and description. An admin will review your application. Once approved you can start listing immediately.

What is the Reseller program?

Once you've spent $100+ total, you unlock reseller status. Resellers can re-list items they've purchased, limited to 1 resale per day.

Is my identity protected?

Yes. Email is optional. Vendors cannot see buyer identities. Buyers cannot see vendor real identities. All communications use anonymous display names.
