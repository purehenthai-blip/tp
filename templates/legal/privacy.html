Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Core principle: We collect the minimum data needed. Anonymity is the default. We never sell your data.

1. What We Collect

  • Username — required to identify your account
  • Email — optional, for account recovery only
  • Crypto wallet addresses — for deposits and payouts
  • Transaction records — required for escrow operation
  • IP address — logged for security only

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • Your real name unless you provide it voluntarily
  • Government ID or KYC documents
  • Payment card information
  • Tracking cookies or third-party analytics

3. Anonymity

Buyers cannot see vendor real identities. Vendors cannot see buyer identities. Only admins can access account data for dispute resolution and security purposes.

4. Data Retention

Data is retained while your account is active. Inactive accounts with under $15 balance are deleted after 14 days. You can request deletion anytime from profile settings.

5. Third Parties

We never sell your data. We may share minimal data with law enforcement when legally required.

6. Cookies

Session cookies only — required to stay logged in. No tracking cookies. No advertising. No third-party analytics.

7. Contact

Open a support ticket for privacy-related requests.
